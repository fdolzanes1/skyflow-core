# SkyFlow-Core

SkyFlow-Core is a platform that helps airlines respond to operational disruptions in seconds, rather than hours.

- **The problem:** Fog, technical failures, or crew shortages can throw flight networks into disarray. Recovery efforts often still rely heavily on manual work—all while under pressure from time constraints, costs, and regulations, such as EU261 (passenger compensation) and EASA rules regarding crew duty periods.
- **The solution:** The system monitors flights and weather in real time, detects the impact of any given event, and automatically recalculates affected routes. An AI "copilot" proposes recovery plans that remain fully compliant with regulations.
- **Human control is maintained:** the AI ​​provides recommendations and explanations, which the planner then approves.
- **Project execution:** The project is developed over 24 weeks, with public weekly deliverables, utilizing technologies well-established in the European market: Java, Node.js, Kafka, Google Cloud, and generative AI.
- **Performance goal:** to recalculate routes in less than 200 milliseconds.
